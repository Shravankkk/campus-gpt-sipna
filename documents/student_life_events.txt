UMANG is SIPNA's annual cultural fest.
UMANG 2026 was scheduled from 11 February to 17 February 2026.
UMANG 2026 included Group Day, Mismatch, Traditional Day, Bollywood Day, Signature Day, arts, DJ night, theatre, variety and fashion show activities.
Vidyotan is a national-level technical symposium at SIPNA.
Vidyotan includes research papers, robotics, LAN gaming, on-spot programming and poster presentation.
HackGenX 2025 was a national-level hackathon at SIPNA.
HackGenX focused on real-world problem solving, teamwork and interaction with experts.

Yuvanand is positioned around Swami Vivekananda, youth empowerment, spirituality and social harmony.
