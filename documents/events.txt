Technical Events: Sipna students participate in technical events such as coding contests, project competitions, seminars, workshops, and hackathons.
Cultural Events: The college encourages cultural participation through music, dance, drama, anchoring, art, and celebration programs.
Workshops: Departments may organize workshops on programming, electronics, civil engineering tools, mechanical design, communication skills, and emerging technologies.
Hackathons: Coding and innovation events help students build problem-solving skills and teamwork.
Guest Lectures: Industry experts, alumni, and faculty members may conduct guest lectures on technical and career topics.
Student Clubs: Students can join technical clubs, cultural groups, sports groups, and department-level student associations.
Sports Activities: Sports activities help students participate in indoor games, outdoor games, fitness events, and inter-college competitions.
Project Exhibitions: Students may present mini projects, final year projects, models, and prototypes during department events.
Conference Activities: Academic conferences and seminars help students learn about research, innovation, and professional communication.
Soft Skills Events: Group discussion, presentation, public speaking, and personality development events help students prepare for placements.
Participation: Students should follow department notices to register for events and competitions.
Teamwork: Events help students improve confidence, leadership, communication, and teamwork.
