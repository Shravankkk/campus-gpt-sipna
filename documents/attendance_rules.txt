Attendance: Sipna students are expected to attend theory classes, practical sessions, tutorials, seminars, and training activities regularly.
Minimum Attendance: Students should maintain the attendance required by the college and university academic rules to remain eligible for examinations.
Practical Attendance: Laboratory and practical attendance is important because engineering subjects include hands-on work and internal assessment.
Medical Leave: If a student is absent because of medical reasons, the student should submit valid documents to the department office.
Low Attendance: Students with low attendance should meet their class teacher, mentor, or department coordinator as early as possible.
Internal Assessment: Attendance, class tests, assignments, practical work, and student participation may be considered during internal assessment.
Class Teacher: The class teacher or mentor is usually the first contact for attendance-related doubts.
Parent Communication: Departments may inform parents or guardians if a student is irregular in classes.
Academic Discipline: Students should reach class on time, maintain classroom discipline, and follow instructions given by faculty members.
Exam Eligibility: Students should confirm exact exam eligibility rules with the department office because rules may change as per university guidelines.
