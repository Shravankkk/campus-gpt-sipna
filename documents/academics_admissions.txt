For undergraduate admission, the candidate should be an Indian National.
For undergraduate engineering admission, Physics and Mathematics are compulsory at HSC or equivalent level.
For undergraduate engineering admission, the minimum aggregate is 45 percent for general candidates and 40 percent for specified reserved, EWS or PwD Maharashtra categories.
Maharashtra candidates use MHT-CET non-zero score for engineering admission.
All India candidates may use JEE Main positive score or applicable CET routes as per current rules.
A diploma route is also listed with 45 percent or 40 percent thresholds depending on category rules.
Common admission documents include SSC marksheet, HSC marksheet, CET or JEE scorecard, leaving certificate, transfer certificate, nationality proof and domicile.
Category students may need caste certificate, caste validity, Non-Creamy Layer certificate, EWS certificate, TFWS income proof, disability documents or defence documents as applicable.
Current fees should be verified from the Fee Regulating Authority notice for the exact admission year.
Old fee figures from earlier years should not be treated as current 2026-27 fees.
Scholarship and fee reimbursement eligibility should be checked through MahaDBT, CET and government rules for the current year.
