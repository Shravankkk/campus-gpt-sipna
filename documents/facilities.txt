Campus Facilities: Sipna provides academic and student support facilities such as classrooms, laboratories, library, auditorium, canteen, hostel, and sports spaces.
Classrooms: Classrooms are used for lectures, tutorials, seminars, presentations, and academic discussions.
Laboratories: Engineering laboratories help students connect theory with practical experiments.
Internet Facility: Computer laboratories and academic areas may provide internet access for learning and project work.
Auditorium: The auditorium is used for seminars, guest lectures, conferences, cultural programs, and important college events.
Canteen: The canteen provides food and refreshment options for students and staff during college hours.
Girls Hostel: Sipna has girls hostel facilities with rooms, security, and basic student support services.
Hostel Internet: Hostel facilities may include Wi-Fi access for students.
Hostel Safety: Hostel safety support may include security, residential supervision, medical help, and emergency support.
Water Facility: Hostel and campus facilities may include drinking water and water purifier arrangements.
Clean Campus: Students are expected to keep classrooms, laboratories, library, hostel, and campus areas clean.
Student Support: Students can contact faculty mentors, department offices, or administrative offices for help related to academics and campus facilities.
Identity Card: Students should carry their college ID card while using campus facilities.
