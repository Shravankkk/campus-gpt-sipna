SIPNA College of Engineering and Technology is located in Amravati, Maharashtra.
SIPNA College of Engineering and Technology is also known as SIPNA COET or SIPNA College of Engineering, Amravati.
SIPNA College was established in July 1999.
SIPNA College is promoted by Sipna Shikshan Prasarak Mandal, also called SSPM.
Sipna Shikshan Prasarak Mandal was established in 1995.
SIPNA college address is In Front of Nemani Godown, Badnera Road, Amravati, Maharashtra 444701.
SIPNA is a private, unaided, co-educational and non-minority engineering institution.
SIPNA became an autonomous institution from Academic Year 2024-25.
SIPNA remains affiliated to Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University, also called SGBAU.
SIPNA states that it is approved by AICTE.
The Maharashtra CET Cell institute code for SIPNA is 01114.
SIPNA states that it has NAAC A+ accreditation.
CSE, E&TC and IT were named in the October 2025 NBA accreditation announcement for another three-year cycle.
