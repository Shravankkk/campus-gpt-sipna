Placement Cell: Sipna has an Industry Institute Interaction and Corporate Relation Cell, also called the III and CR Cell.
Career Planning: The III and CR Cell helps students with career planning, personality development, and industry readiness.
Campus Interviews: The placement cell coordinates campus interviews and recruitment drives for eligible students.
Training Activities: Students may receive training in aptitude, communication skills, group discussion, interview skills, and personality development.
Industrial Exposure: The cell supports industrial visits, implant training, guest lectures, and interaction with industry professionals.
Internships: Students are encouraged to participate in internships and practical training to understand real industry work.
Communication Skills: Language and communication training helps students improve confidence for interviews and workplace interaction.
Resume Preparation: Students should prepare a clean resume with education details, projects, internships, technical skills, and achievements.
Mock Interviews: Mock interviews and group discussion practice help students prepare for company selection rounds.
Eligible Students: Final year and pre-final year students should follow placement notices shared by the department or placement cell.
Departments: Placement activities may include students from Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics and Telecommunication, Civil, Mechanical, and MBA.
Company Visits: Companies may visit the campus or conduct online recruitment depending on their hiring process.
Student Responsibility: Students should check placement announcements regularly and complete registrations before deadlines.
