SIPNA operates a DGCA authorised Remote Pilot Training Organisation activity, also called RPTO.
SIPNA states that RPTO operations commenced in June 2024.
SIPNA describes its RPTO as the first such institute in Vidarbha, presented as an institutional claim.
The RPTO course is described as a 5-day DGCA-approved training programme.
The RPTO course structure is 2 days theory, 1 day lab or simulator and 2 days flying.
RPTO eligibility shown includes at least 10th pass or equivalent, age 18 to 65, valid Indian passport and medical certificate.
RPTO topics include DGCA rules, drone fundamentals, operations, maintenance, payload, data analysis, emergencies, weather and ATC or radiotelephony.
